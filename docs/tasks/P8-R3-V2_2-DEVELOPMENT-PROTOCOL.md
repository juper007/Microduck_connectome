Preserve the [official V2.1 summary](../evidence/p8-r3/official-v21-v1-summary.md), batch summary SHA256 `b0a2b72e4b798cfea8719f863d8eefae84b834851dab3622ec5d590d72df82db`, and raw manifest SHA256 `7ad1497762a6f081a32fdb37bc9d7a11797eee17e32ad92f57b31cc4ac7d89dd` as negative evidence.

**V2.2 hypothesis:** at a prospectively selected **20 Hz RGB cadence**, the already selected fractional representation and estimator can remain fresh under the 100 ms TTL and produce healthy preboundary neural stops in moving official trials. Nominal RGB spacing is 50 ms, leaving 50 ms nominal margin to TTL; actual scheduling and processing jitter must be measured, not assumed away. A separate fault-arbitration repair must keep a stop latched after transient staleness and preserve fault priority. A healthy neural stop may never be inferred from a fault stop. These are engineering timing and safety hypotheses, not biological claims.

The planned functional changes are (1) selecting **20 Hz**, rather than V2.1's 10 Hz, in the official visual scheduler and (2) correcting stop arbitration so an authentic fault or neural stop remains a stop when a later watchdog output becomes non-stop. Fault must supersede neural stop in the effective fault/arbiter latch reason and transport priority. Record a separate code/config diff and tests for each change.

Keep the existing complete-lineage limit of **≤5** 50 Hz neural steps from latest positive looming/LPLC2 to first DN stop.

Once the first authentic watchdog safe stop or healthy neural stop latches, every subsequent robot-facing publication through pose-confirmed completion or deadline must be `robot.stop` at the production 50 Hz refresh cadence (maximum request/ACK refresh gap **100 ms**), with **zero positive `robot.move` requests or ACKs and zero scheduler exceptions**. Include fault while a move ACK is in flight, neural-then-fault priority, repeated fault refresh ACKs, and an attempted non-stop watchdog output after latch. The effective fault/arbiter latch reason must outrank a neural reason; preserve watchdog first-fault history separately. A fault-triggered stopped body is a safety result only; it does not pass the looming gate. Record the test source/head, event ledger, command/ACK order, and outcome. A failed gate blocks official trials; no selective deletion of fault cases.
