## P8-02 — approach success and causality

For each of 20 unique fresh resets, use the P8-01 virtual approaching sphere at **0.20 m/s**, V2.4 geometry, and the listed arm time. Establish read-only MuJoCo trunk displacement/speed and fresh positive robotd applied vx before neural arm. Keep valid RGB and neural updates continuous through pose-derived cessation. A success requires, in strict time order: graded RGB → fractional target area → positive A looming → LPLC2 → healthy graph-v2 DNp01 **≥0.5** → EscapeDecoder stop → SafetyClamp → healthy Watchdog → neural stop latch → first `robot.stop` request/ACK → applied-vx decline → pose-derived stopped confirmation before deadman or cleanup. The **first healthy neural stop and first stop request** must each have a **strictly positive** `center distance − 0.25 m` margin while the body is still moving. Require full original-source visual lineage (no armed `input_none`, `result_none`, invalid frame, stale source age, or graph fault), actual RGB interframe gaps and every neural frame age **≤100 ms**, lineage within **five** 50 Hz runtime steps, ACK refresh gap **≤100 ms**, zero positive `robot.move` after latch, and no scripted/zero-twist/fault/deadman/shutdown cessation confound. A safe fault stop is **not** a looming success. Late/absent stop, transport or stopped-confirmation failure, or any armed missing input counts as a failure in the fixed 20 denominator.

For **every** approach attempt, record first positive looming, LPLC2, DN threshold, healthy neural stop, stop request, ACK, applied-vx reduction/near-zero, and pose-confirmed stop times; evaluator center distance and boundary margin at each; moving-body pose speed and fresh applied vx; RGB/neural health and lineage; command/ACK stream; and all safety/fault events. Derive looming→neural-stop, neural-stop→request, neural-stop→ACK, ACK→stopped, and looming→stopped latencies in elapsed milliseconds. Report per-seed results, failures by cause, median, nearest-rank p95, maximum, and **Wilson 95% CI** for the success proportion. Separately report the pose-confirmed preboundary-stop proportion and minimum virtual surface clearance as **secondary, non-gating** safety outcomes. No positive confirmed-stop margin is imputed from a preboundary trigger.

## P8-03 — actual moving-body false-positive controls

Use 20 static and 20 receding **nonapproaching relative-image trajectories** on independent fresh official resets with the same moving-body precondition, RGB representation, graph/decoder/safety, arm cycle, 20 Hz/50 Hz schedule, and **1,000 ms** scored window after arm. A world-fixed sphere would become optically approaching as the robot walks; it is not a valid static looming control. Therefore the **synthetic virtual center is recomputed from each measured trunk pose** along camera forward bearing so evaluator center distance remains **0.85 m** for static or follows **`0.85 + 0.20 × elapsed seconds after arm` m** for receding. The camera-relative 0.85 m baseline is established before arm with no area jump at the scoring transition. This fixture motion is an explicit engineering control, not a biological or physical obstacle claim. The scorer uses evaluator-only truth; the controller sees only RGB/ToF. Log per-frame actual relative distance, image area, bearing, pose, and fixture trajectory. Static range must stay within **±0.005 m** and receding frame-to-frame range change must be **≥−0.005 m**. A post-arm geometry violation is a **scored trial failure**, never a hidden exclusion or clean true negative.

A false positive is **any healthy DNp01/EscapeDecoder neural stop or neural-origin `robot.stop`** inside the full scored window before planned cleanup, whether or not pose cessation completes. A fault stop, deadman stop, stale sensor/neural input, fixture violation, or scheduler exception is **not** a clean true negative; retain it as a failed/contaminated trial and block batch PASS rather than removing it from the denominator. Report static, receding, and pooled rates with per-seed rows and Wilson 95% CIs. Require **≤1** false neural stop in each set of 20 and **≤2** in 40 pooled, **all 40 cleanly accounted**, and zero safety-limit violations. Planned end-of-window cleanup is labeled separately and cannot erase an earlier false stop.
