Preserve v1 estimator behavior. Do not lower the decoder threshold, alter graph gain/weights, or enlarge the boundary in this comparison.

For detected fractional area `a` in `[0,1]`, valid strictly increasing timestamps, and `dt` in seconds, compare these versioned V2 candidates at 10 Hz. `eps = 1e-6` in all three. Each returns `clamp(rate/full_scale, 0, 1)` and zero for nonpositive rate. Invalid/no-target or zero area clears history and returns neutral; the first subsequent valid sample establishes a baseline and returns zero. An interframe gap above 150 ms clears history; the arriving sample becomes the new baseline with output zero. These values come from the prospectively specified design; no P8-R3 output may be used to adjust them within this protocol.

| Candidate | Frozen rate definition | Full scale | State |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| A: relative area | `[ln(a_t + eps) - ln(a_prev + eps)] / dt` | 0.5 s⁻¹ | Previous valid area/time |
| B: relative radius | `[sqrt(a_t + eps) - sqrt(a_prev + eps)] / [sqrt(a_prev + eps) × dt]` | 0.25 s⁻¹ | Previous valid area/time |
| C: short regression | Ordinary least-squares slope of `ln(a + eps)` against time over the trailing 200 ms, including the current sample | 0.5 s⁻¹ | Window of valid samples |

Candidate B is approximately half of A for small area increments. Its finite difference and early quantization behavior can differ, but A and B must **not** be presented as independent biological mechanisms. For C, fewer than two valid window samples return zero; window membership and output timestamp use the current frame. The implementation and tests must match these definitions before results are inspected.

## Paired internal screen

Use development-only approach seeds **885301, 885302, 885303**, respectively with virtual arm elapsed times **2.0, 2.6, 3.0 s**. For every A/B/C candidate, render the same approach at **65×33 and 129×65** with identical world geometry, detector, 10 Hz timestamps, neural initial state, graph-v2, decoder, and safety configuration. Evaluate static and receding controls at each resolution with the same three seed/arm combinations. The six approach and twelve control cases per candidate are a fixed matrix. An optional **97×49** interpolation check uses the same seeds and is reported separately; it cannot replace either required resolution. Record every case, including failures.

The deterministic internal causal path is RGB → camera detector → V2 estimator → PerceptionFrame → SensoryMapper → LPLC2 → graph-v2 → DNp01 → EscapeDecoder. The replay is a fixed-pose/virtual-approach neural screen; it cannot establish official moving-body stop success. Record first positive looming time and boundary margin, peak looming, positive-frame count, peak LPLC2 and positive steps, DNp01 peak and first threshold time/margin, first healthy neural stop time/margin, runtime health, and all nonfinite/invalid events. The margin is evaluator center distance minus 0.25 m. Strictly positive margin means before the boundary.

An encoder passes the internal neural gate only if, at **each** required resolution, all **3/3** approaches reach DNp01 ≥ 0.5 and produce a healthy EscapeDecoder neural stop at strictly positive predicted boundary margin, while **0/6** static and receding cases at that resolution produce a healthy neural stop. All input/runtime states must remain valid and finite. This screen is deliberately stricter than a pooled 3/3 across resolutions.

Resolution comparability is evaluated on each paired approach seed, before selection: first-positive looming times and first DN threshold times must each differ by at most **one 10 Hz visual period (100 ms)** between 65×33 and 129×65, and the ratio of their peak LPLC2 amplitudes (`129×65 / 65×33`) must lie in **[0.75, 1.333]**. A pair with zero peak cannot meet this ratio. These engineering tolerances test approximate timing and amplitude invariance; report the continuous differences and ratios as well as pass/fail. The same margins and neural gate remain primary if a candidate has similar amplitudes but insufficient drive.

Select **one** candidate prospectively by this ordered rule: (1) internal neural and control gate; (2) resolution comparability on all three pairs; (3) greatest minimum first-neural-stop boundary margin across the six approaches; (4) lowest maximum first-neural-stop latency from the first positive looming frame; (5) simplest state/computation, A before B before C if otherwise tied. If none passes, report P8-R3 internal FAIL and design a newly versioned hypothesis with new development seeds; do not move a failed candidate to Thor. Keep every negative result and all formula/parameter versions.

## Validity, failure, and evidence accounting

An invalid official reset is limited to an exogenous failure **before neural arm** that prevents a comparable trial, such as simulator startup/IPC failure, absent confirmed pre-arm movement, or malformed fixture setup. Preserve its raw logs and reason. A started, armed trial with valid motion and RGB that lacks a threshold crossing or stop is a **FAIL**, not invalid. A fault, boundary crossing, late stop, deadman, transport gap, safety violation, or failed stopped confirmation after arm is also a reported failure or safety event. If a harness defect requires a code/protocol change, freeze a new version and rerun the complete affected three-reset matrix with newly designated development seeds; never silently replace only unfavorable rows. Repeated pre-arm invalidity blocks recertification until the infrastructure is repaired and independently reviewed.

Publish per-seed rows, selected and rejected candidate rationale, all control/rate results, internal and official raw artifact paths, source and config hashes, SHA256/byte/record manifest, simulator cleanup outcome, and exact reproduction commands under `docs/evidence/p8-r3/`. An independent reviewer must inspect the final branch head, seed disjointness, prospective freeze, unchanged graph/decoder/boundary, controls, causal stop order, official 3/3 criterion, raw hashes, and current-main freshness before merge. Do not freeze P8-v2 final protocol until P8-R3 is reviewed PASS and merged.
