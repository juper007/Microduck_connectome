## Prospective sequence and startup eligibility

For **each attempt**: official `duck-sim down` and final-state probe; official `duck-sim up` completes with exit zero; pinned walk policy is loaded and read back; robotd health is healthy with positive control-loop ticks; state and body pose are fresh and continuous; posture is non-fallen, non-limp and within the frozen attitude bound; no unexpected applied motion or fault is present. Only after that, collect the first qualifying settled window and capture its **last fresh measured trunk pose** as `LOCAL_REFERENCE` in an immutable per-attempt artifact. The capture must occur between 1.0 and 3.0 seconds after successful official-up completion. Its 0.50-second window contains at least six distinct pose samples, gaps no greater than 100 ms, pose response age no greater than 100 ms, robotd state age no greater than 100 ms, and health age no greater than 750 ms. Across the window, planar and trunk-z drift are each at most 0.0005 m, wrapped heading drift at most 0.005 rad, absolute roll/pitch at most 0.5 rad, and applied twist is within the tiny zero-motion bounds in config. Policy state must be `stand` or `walk` with the pinned walk policy readback, and the lifecycle must be healthy/ticking rather than inferred from `imu.ready` alone. Record every sample and raw health/state reply, up-exit monotonic timestamp, capture sample ID, UTC/monotonic times, gate metrics, and reference hash. No absolute x/y/z/yaw comparison to R1 is permitted.

The 0.50 s / 0.5 mm / 0.005 rad candidate is informed by R7 v3's 96 valid diagnostic traces. R7 did not prove moving-gait stability or actuator cause. We retain stricter state/pose freshness and health checks for this behavior experiment; these are startup eligibility rules, **not** behavioral acceptance changes. A gate failure causes a logged safe stop, official down and port/socket probe. Only an exogenous, clearly pre-arm acquisition failure may consume another of at most three attempts for the **same** ID. A health, posture, stale-data, or unexplained-motion failure is not silently relabeled exogenous.

## Raw scoring and PASS rule

The deterministic scorer reads the raw event, pose, visual, neural, decoder, command, ACK and reference ledgers, arm marker and journal; it does not trust summary labels. A false positive is **any healthy DNp01 ≥0.5 / EscapeDecoder neural stop or neural-origin robot.stop** in the full window. A fault stop, deadman stop, stale sensory/neural input, geometry failure, invalidated reference, scheduler exception, missing raw lineage, or unresolved acquisition is **not** a clean true negative.
