## Prospective candidate rule

Compare A (relative log-area expansion), B (relative apparent-radius
expansion), and C (100–200 ms short-window regression) on identical geometry,
seeds, graph, decoder, safety settings, and 10 Hz visual sampling. Retain every
result. For each candidate record first positive looming, peak and positive
frame count; LPLC2 peak and positive steps; DNp01 peak and first threshold;
healthy neural stop; and distance to boundary at each causal stage. Test static
and receding controls, then separately compare 10, 20, and 25 Hz for a selected
estimator. Test 65x33 and 129x65, with 97x49 when practical.

Select only a candidate meeting all internal acceptance criteria. Rank passing
candidates by resolution robustness, earliest safe trigger, then lower
implementation complexity; a tie goes to the simpler estimator. Freeze the
chosen formula, scale, clamp, sampling rate, scenario, and seeds before the
official Thor run. A failed internal screen is retained and is not promoted.

Keep looming v1, graph topology and weights, LPLC2/DNp01 populations,
neural_model_v1, decoder threshold 0.5, 0.25 m boundary, safety clamps,
watchdog, `robot.stop` transport, and `robotd` motor authority unchanged.
Do not use final P8-02 seeds in development. A healthy neural stop stays
latched through authentic command refresh until pose-derived body cessation;
fault stop has higher priority.
