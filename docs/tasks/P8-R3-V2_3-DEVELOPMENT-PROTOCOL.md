## Retained result and single new hypothesis

The V2.2 internal 18-case synthetic screen and separate fault-stop gate passed. Its official Thor batch at source `0d1a178767cf4eec5ba5e53b464e4658d4fbfca9`, seeds **885521–885523**, is permanently **0/3 FAIL**. All three bodies moved and produced healthy preboundary neural stops, stop ACKs, and pose-confirmed cessation with zero safety violations or scheduler exceptions. But each armed neural ledger contains **4/3/2** early `input_none=true, result_none=true` ticks after the directly invoked neutral priming update. Valid updates had maximum visual ages **60.35/63.69/64.05 ms** and frame gaps **69.76/60.00/60.00 ms**, but those maxima exclude the skipped ticks. Thus `visual_age_within_ttl` was false in every run; the completed trials may not be reclassified or selectively replaced. Preserve the [official V2.2 summary](../evidence/p8-r3/official-v22-v1-summary.md), batch summary SHA256 `12d6b52cc612285a1a5439880b46912dc7f838ff184bb92030462e92bf6e1974`, raw manifest SHA256 `66500298b36c1ded6d5c341117a009f36deeb17bb8b899ba5f0f156f7f6c64ca`, and all 32 verified raw files.

The isolated handoff cause is that the first validated RGB/PerceptionFrame was passed directly to `chain.neural` and Watchdog for neutral priming **before** scheduler threads started, while the scheduler's perception cache remained empty. Its early neural ticks therefore received `None` until the next visual publication. **V2.3 hypothesis:** publishing that same validated priming frame, with its original source timestamp, into the scheduler perception cache before starting the scheduler will eliminate all armed skipped neural ticks while retaining the unchanged 100 ms TTL and the healthy V2.2 looming and stop behavior. This is an engineering startup handoff repair, not a biological claim.

It must accept only the already validated first PerceptionFrame used for the existing neutral graph/Watchdog priming; preserve its frame ID and original camera timestamp; publish it to the scheduler cache **before** any scheduler worker can read that cache; and reject absent, invalid, future-dated, or already-stale input.
