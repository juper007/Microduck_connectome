# P8-03-R7: official SIT startup interval instrumentation

## Purpose and separation

R7 instruments only the startup interval that R6 localized between robotd reachability (C) and completion of official `duck-sim up` (A). R1–R5 terminal FAIL PRs and Releases and R6 PASS PR/Release remain unchanged. R6 did not pass P8-03. R7 is development only and neither changes the final reference nor authorizes final P8 IDs, 60-reset qualification, correction, or P8-04.

## Version history and prospective v2 matrix

The frozen v1 protocol used labels `888400`–`888495`. Its first Thor attempt stopped at `888400` with terminal `SAFETY_FAIL` because the first `robot.health` poll returned `healthy=false`, reason `control loop has not completed a cycle yet`, `control_loop.ticks=0`, `imu.ready=false`, and no `degraded` field. The v1 evidence Release is retained unchanged as terminal FAIL. That observation does not prove an unsafe post-startup state; it identifies a pre-cycle health response that v1 treated as immediately unsafe. None of the unused v1 labels is carried into v2.

The separately versioned v2 protocol preregisters 96 fresh independent official SIT down/up resets, `T00`–`T95`, labeled `888500`–`888595` in twelve ordered blocks of eight. These are unique labels for one-attempt accounting and evidence indexing, **not simulator RNG seeds**. The pinned official body server exposes no RNG-seed input. No reset is retried or replaced. Outside-final-envelope poses are retained observations and never terminate collection. The v2 evidence tag is `p8-03-r7-startup-interval-evidence-v2`.

The runner sends no `robot.move`, no explicit `robot.enable`, no policy load, and no correction. The pinned official `duck-sim up` command itself invokes robotd enable to hand the duck to its stand policy. R7 observes that lifecycle. The only direct robot command from R7 is a bounded, acknowledged `robot.stop` for safety or cleanup.

## Continuous raw capture

Launch an independent official `duck-sim down`/`up` for each label. Immediately after the body server handshake, begin body reads targeting 50 Hz; keep the read loop alive through `UP_EXIT` and at least one second afterward. Capture continues to a prospectively defined stable 0.5-second window, up to three seconds after up exits. The measured body-sample gap must never exceed 100 ms. A separate reader persists **every** `robot.state` notification, targeting the official 50 Hz subscription, while a third worker checks health every 0.2 seconds. Health RPC or robotd connection attempts never block body reads. Each body sample contains a socket probe, full raw official packet, parsed trunk pose, quaternion, heading/roll/pitch, simulator time, joint positions/velocities/currents where supplied, and links to the latest separately retained robotd state and health. Raw robotd state includes policy, requested and applied **high-level twist**, `limited_by`, safety, robotd time, joints, and targets. Contact/gait is retained if the pinned packet exposes it; absent fields remain explicit null. Timestamp official up output lines on receipt with the same host monotonic clock used for body and robotd rows. Keep each source's own time as well.

Every journal row is appended and fsynced before any safety or event classification. The robotd reader first persists each raw wire notification, including malformed lines, before parsing it or attaching a parsed state. A journal-wide sequential index identifies every source row; body and robotd state notifications additionally have continuous per-stream indices. The standalone scorer reopens retained raw packets and state JSON, reconstructs pose and event timestamps, verifies source links and actual sample gaps, and compares its derived metrics against the runner's trace. Body onset and gaps use body response timestamps; policy and applied events use robotd notification receipt timestamps; up exit uses its captured observation timestamp. Later journal timestamps prove persistence order but cannot reorder the source events. The runner adds explicit timeline records after raw capture; the scorer requires exact agreement with independent derivation.

During v2 startup only, the single allowlisted pre-cycle `robot.health` tuple is `healthy=false`, reason exactly `control loop has not completed a cycle yet`, `control_loop.ticks=0`, `imu.ready=false`, and `degraded` absent. It may be observed for at most five seconds after `ROBOTD_REACHABLE`, provided the contemporaneous raw state and body samples remain safe and well formed. Each poll is retained and classified as `STARTUP_UNREADY`, never silently counted as healthy. The grace period ends at the first healthy control cycle and cannot restart within that reset. Any other unhealthy/degraded response, any safety contradiction in state or pose, persistence beyond five seconds, or return to unready after a healthy cycle stops the matrix with the appropriate safety or data-integrity failure. The full health/state timeline remains required to PASS.

## Prospective event rules

Use the first fresh body sample as the SIT baseline. `FIRST_POSE_DEVIATION` is the first of two consecutive fresh-simulator-time samples with planar displacement at least 0.002 m, absolute z change at least 0.002 m, **or** wrapped heading change at least 0.02 rad from that baseline. `FIRST_NONZERO_APPLIED` is the first of two consecutive distinct robotd state timestamps with any applied twist component magnitude greater than 0.0001. The first candidate and confirmed event are recoverable from raw rows; an absent event is `NOT_OBSERVED`, never fabricated. These thresholds diagnose startup timing only. The unchanged final P8 inclusion limits remain x/y 0.03 m, z 0.025 m, heading 0.08 rad around the frozen reference, used only to classify the settled endpoint.

Required explicit timeline names are `BODY_REACHABLE`, `ROBOTD_REACHABLE`, `STARTUP_UNREADY`, `FIRST_HEALTHY_CYCLE`, `POLICY_HELD`, `POLICY_STAND`, `FIRST_NONZERO_APPLIED`, `FIRST_POSE_DEVIATION`, `UP_EXIT`, and `STAND_SETTLED`. The policy state must be observed in the raw notification stream. Each observed event names its source journal row, host monotonic timestamp, and bounded onset interval from the preceding sample. `STARTUP_UNREADY` may be `NOT_OBSERVED` if robotd is ready at the first health poll; `FIRST_HEALTHY_CYCLE` is required. Order is assigned only when those intervals do not overlap. The official process `standing up` log line is retained as a separately timestamped lifecycle clue, not an exact actuator timestamp.

`STAND_SETTLED` is the earliest post-up window beginning at least 0.5 seconds after up exit, spanning at least 0.44 seconds of samples, and ending no earlier than one second after up exit, during which all poses remain within 0.5 mm planar, 0.5 mm z, and 0.005 rad heading of its first sample. The raw scorer recomputes it. R7 describes C→A displacement using the first body sample after the first robotd state and the first body sample after `UP_EXIT`. It also fixes before/after/overlap comparisons among policy stand, first nonzero applied high-level twist, and first pose deviation. Outlier versus inside-envelope summaries compare onset lag, peak planar/heading excursion, and peak planar speed; these are descriptive trajectory contrasts. No stop or correction is sent during capture.

## Gate, interpretation, and evidence

The matrix stops on genuine fallen/limp/unhealthy robotd outside the narrow v2 pre-cycle allowance, body attitude over 0.5 rad, applied twist beyond the frozen R6 safety limits, malformed/nonfinite/stale or gapped raw data, failed official lifecycle, or failed cleanup. The triggering raw row remains durable before stop/down. Out-of-envelope pose never triggers a stop. A partial matrix is FAIL, remains intact, and is published without retry.

R7 PASS requires all 96 preregistered attempts, complete high-rate body/robotd/raw lifecycle timelines, all mandatory event derivations and crosschecks, and successful cleanup. `FIRST_NONZERO_APPLIED` may be `NOT_OBSERVED` when the complete state stream supports that conclusion. An observational class may recommend the next remediation target; it does **not** establish actuator causality. A zero high-level applied twist does not rule out official stand-policy joint actuation. Compare normal and final-envelope-outlier trajectories using the frozen classification; if no outlier recurs, report `NOT_REPRODUCED`, without extending the matrix.
